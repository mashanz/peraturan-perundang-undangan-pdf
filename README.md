# 🏛️ Indonesian Legal Database - PDF Archive

**Professional PDF Repository for Indonesian Legal Regulations**  
*Organized in standardized 8-tier hierarchical structure*

## 🔗 Related Repository
**Main Repository (Markdown):** https://github.com/mashanz/peraturan-perundang-undangan

## 📁 Directory Structure

### **01-CONSTITUTIONAL** - Supreme Legal Documents
Constitutional foundations, MPR decrees, and constitutional court decisions

### **02-NATIONAL-LAWS** - Parliamentary Legislation
Laws (UU), emergency regulations (PERPPU), and national legal codes

### **03-GOVERNMENT-REGULATIONS** - Executive Regulations
Government regulations (PP), presidential regulations (PERPRES), and instructions (INPRES)

### **04-MINISTERIAL** - Ministry Implementation Rules
Ministerial regulations organized by ministry and year, including Finance, Transportation, Energy, Education, Health, and others

### **05-REGIONAL** - Local Government Regulations
Provincial (PERDA) and regency/city regulations

### **06-JUDICIAL** - Court Decisions and Judicial Rules
Supreme Court and Constitutional Court decisions

### **07-SPECIALIZED** - Independent Institution Regulations
Regulations from specialized agencies like BPK (Audit Agency) and KPU (Election Commission)

### **08-ARCHIVES** - Historical Backup and Legacy Structure
Complete backup of original structure and chronological organization
