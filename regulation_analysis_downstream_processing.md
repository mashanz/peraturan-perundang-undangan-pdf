# DETAILED REGULATION ANALYSIS: DOWNSTREAM PROCESSING & RAW MATERIALS

## REGULATION 7: RAW MATERIAL EXPORT RESTRICTIONS (HILIRISASI POLICY)
**Legal Basis:** Peraturan Menteri Perindustrian tentang Larangan Ekspor Bahan Baku Mineral
**Status:** Active - Progressive Implementation 2024-2025
**Priority Level:** STRATEGIC NATIONAL IMPORTANCE

### LEGAL TEXT ANALYSIS
**Foundational Principles:**
- Maximum value addition through domestic processing
- Industrial development acceleration
- Employment creation through manufacturing
- National economic sovereignty enhancement

**Covered Raw Materials:**
- **Nickel Ore:** Export ban since 2020, refined nickel products allowed
- **Bauxite:** Progressive export restrictions, alumina processing required
- **Copper Concentrate:** Smelting requirement for export permit
- **Tin Ore:** Processing requirement for value-added products
- **Coal:** Domestic market obligation (DMO) 25% minimum

### IMPLEMENTATION FRAMEWORK
**Phase 1: Notification and Preparation (12 months)**
- Industry stakeholder consultation
- Investment promotion for processing facilities
- Technical assistance for smelter development
- Infrastructure readiness assessment

**Phase 2: Gradual Implementation (24 months)**
- Progressive export quota reduction
- Processing facility development monitoring
- Investment realization tracking
- Employment impact assessment

**Phase 3: Full Implementation**
- Complete raw material export prohibition
- Value-added product export promotion
- Domestic supply chain optimization
- International market penetration

### DOWNSTREAM PROCESSING REQUIREMENTS
**Minimum Processing Standards:**
- **Nickel:** Class 1 nickel (>99.8% purity) or nickel chemicals
- **Bauxite:** Alumina (Al2O3) minimum 98% purity
- **Copper:** Copper cathode or copper products
- **Tin:** Tin ingots or tin-based alloys
- **Coal:** Coal derivatives or coal-to-chemical products

**Investment Requirements:**
- Minimum investment thresholds per commodity
- Technology transfer obligations
- Local workforce development
- Environmental compliance standards
- Export performance commitments

**Downstream Integration:**

## REGULATION 8: IMPORT SUBSTITUTION STRATEGY
**Legal Basis:** Peraturan tentang Strategi Substitusi Impor Industri
**Status:** Active - Implementation 2023-2025
**Priority Level:** HIGH

### STRATEGIC IMPORT SUBSTITUTION SECTORS
**Priority Industries:**
1. **Petrochemicals:** Basic chemicals, polymers, specialty chemicals
2. **Steel and Metal Products:** Hot-rolled steel, steel pipes, metal components
3. **Machinery and Equipment:** Industrial machinery, agricultural equipment
4. **Electronics Components:** Semiconductors, circuit boards, sensors
5. **Pharmaceutical Raw Materials:** Active pharmaceutical ingredients (APIs)

### IMPLEMENTATION MECHANISMS
**Local Content Requirements:**
- Progressive increase: 30% (Year 1) → 50% (Year 3) → 70% (Year 5)
- Calculation methodology: Value-added basis
- Verification procedures: Third-party audit
