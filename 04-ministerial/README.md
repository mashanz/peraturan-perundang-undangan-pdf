# 04-MINISTERIAL - Ministerial Regulations
## 🏛️ Ministry-Level Implementation Regulations

This directory contains regulations issued by individual ministries to implement higher-level laws and government regulations.

### Directory Structure
- **by-ministry/** - Organized by issuing ministry
  - **kemenkeu/** - Ministry of Finance (PMK) - 📊 **LARGEST COLLECTION**
  - **kemenhub/** - Ministry of Transportation (Permen Hub)
  - **kemen-esdm/** - Ministry of Energy and Mineral Resources
  - **kemendikbud/** - Ministry of Education and Culture
  - **kemkes/** - Ministry of Health
  - **kemenhan/** - Ministry of Defense
  - **bumn/** - State-Owned Enterprises Ministry
  - **other-ministries/** - Other ministerial regulations
- **by-year/** - Cross-ministry chronological organization

### Document Types by Ministry
- **PMK**: Finance Ministry Regulations (Peraturan Menteri Keuangan)
- **Permen Hub**: Transportation Ministry Regulations
- **Permen ESDM**: Energy & Mineral Resources Regulations
- **Permen other**: Various ministry-specific regulations

### Hierarchy Level: **LEVEL 4** ⭐⭐
Technical implementing regulations with specific sectoral authority.
