# KEMENKEU THEMATIC POLICY ANALYSIS
## Deep Thematic Analysis of Indonesian Ministry of Finance Regulations

**Analysis Date:** February 18, 2026  
**Scope:** Cross-year thematic analysis across all major policy domains  
**Methodology:** Regulatory gap analysis, international benchmarking, policy evolution tracking

---

## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

This comprehensive analysis examines Indonesia's Ministry of Finance regulatory framework across seven critical domains, identifying policy evolution patterns, implementation gaps, and modernization opportunities. The analysis reveals significant progress in tax administration digitization and customs modernization, while highlighting gaps in digital finance regulation and regional finance coordination mechanisms.

**Key Findings:**
- **Digital Transformation**: Accelerating across tax (SIAP system) and customs (National Single Window)
- **Regulatory Gaps**: Digital finance, cryptocurrency, and fintech oversight frameworks
- **Modernization Priority**: Regional finance transfer mechanisms and debt management frameworks
- **International Alignment**: Strong in tax policy, moderate in customs, developing in digital finance
