# PP LEVEL 4 CONVERSION: ENVIRONMENTAL GOVERNANCE AND CLIMATE CHANGE ADAPTATION

## **PP No. 22/2021 + Updates - Environmental Protection and Climate Change Management Framework**

### **COMPLETE PP TEXT ANALYSIS**

**Official Title:** Peraturan Pemerintah tentang Penyelenggaraan Perlindungan dan Pengelolaan Lingkungan Hidup serta Adaptasi dan Mitigasi Perubahan Iklim

**Legal Foundation:** UU No. 32/2009 (Environmental Protection), Paris Agreement ratification, UU No. 16/2016 (Climate Change), Presidential Regulation on Green Development

### **CORE REGULATORY FRAMEWORK**

#### **Integrated Environmental Governance (Pasal 1-40)**

1. **Climate Change Mitigation and Adaptation Framework**
   - National greenhouse gas emission reduction targets (29% unconditional, 41% conditional by 2030)
   - Regional climate adaptation planning integration with development planning
   - Carbon pricing mechanism implementation and monitoring
   - Renewable energy transition mandatory targets by sector and region

2. **Environmental Impact Assessment Integration**
   - **Streamlined EIA Process:** Risk-based environmental assessment
   - **Strategic Environmental Assessment (SEA):** Mandatory for regional development plans
   - **Digital Environmental Monitoring:** Real-time air, water, and soil quality tracking
   - **Community-based Monitoring:** Citizen participation in environmental surveillance

3. **Circular Economy Implementation**
   - Waste management hierarchy enforcement (reduce, reuse, recycle)
   - Extended producer responsibility for packaging and electronics
   - Regional waste management integration and resource recovery
   - Plastic waste reduction targets and alternative material promotion

### **CLIMATE GOVERNANCE INTEGRATION**

**NATIONAL LEVEL COORDINATION:**

**REGIONAL LEVEL IMPLEMENTATION:**

### **SECTORAL INTEGRATION REQUIREMENTS**

#### **Green Economy Transition Framework**

**ENERGY SECTOR TRANSFORMATION:**
- **Renewable Energy Targets:** 23% by 2025, 31% by 2050
- **Coal Phase-Out Schedule:** No new coal plants after 2023, gradual retirement of existing plants
- **Energy Efficiency Standards:** 1% annual energy intensity improvement
- **Green Energy Certification:** Renewable energy certificate trading system

**TRANSPORTATION DECARBONIZATION:**
- **Electric Vehicle Adoption:** 20% of new vehicle sales by 2030
- **Public Transportation Electrification:** 100% electric bus fleet in major cities by 2030
- **Sustainable Fuel Standards:** Biofuel blending mandates and aviation sustainable fuel requirements
- **Active Transportation Infrastructure:** Cycling and walking infrastructure investment requirements

**INDUSTRIAL ENVIRONMENTAL STANDARDS:**
- **Cleaner Production Implementation:** Mandatory for high-impact industries
- **Circular Economy Targets:** 30% waste reduction and 50% recycling rate by 2030
- **Green Building Standards:** Mandatory green building certification for public buildings
- **Environmental Management System:** ISO 14001 certification requirements for large enterprises

### **ENFORCEMENT AND SANCTIONS FRAMEWORK**

#### **Environmental Compliance Assurance**

**GRADUATED ENFORCEMENT APPROACH:**
1. **Administrative Measures:** Warning letters, improvement orders, permit suspension
2. **Economic Penalties:** Financial sanctions proportional to environmental damage
3. **Criminal Enforcement:** Prosecution for serious environmental crimes
4. **Civil Liability:** Environmental damage compensation and restoration requirements

**CORPORATE ENVIRONMENTAL RESPONSIBILITY:**
- **Mandatory Environmental Reporting:** Annual sustainability report requirement for listed companies
- **Environmental Due Diligence:** Environmental risk assessment in business operations
- **Supply Chain Environmental Standards:** Upstream and downstream environmental compliance requirements
- **Executive Environmental Liability:** Personal responsibility for corporate environmental violations
