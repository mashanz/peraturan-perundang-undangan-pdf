# PP LEVEL 4 CONVERSION: INVESTMENT LICENSING REFORM (OSS SYSTEM)

## **PP No. 5/2021 + Updates - Investment Licensing Reform and OSS Implementation**

### **COMPLETE PP TEXT ANALYSIS**

**Official Title:** Peraturan Pemerintah tentang Penyelenggaraan Perizinan dan Investasi Terintegrasi Secara Elektronik (OSS Implementation)

**Legal Foundation:** UU No. 11/2020 (Job Creation Law), UU No. 25/2007 (Investment Law), Presidential Regulation on Investment Coordination

### **CORE REGULATORY FRAMEWORK**

#### **OSS System Architecture (Pasal 1-25)**

1. **Integrated Licensing Platform**
   - Single portal for all business licensing (oss.go.id)
   - Risk-based licensing classification (Low, Medium-Low, Medium-High, High)
   - Real-time status tracking and notification system
   - Automatic license issuance for qualified applications

2. **Streamlined Business Process**
   - **Pre-Investment Phase:** NIB (Nomor Induk Berusaha) issuance in maximum 4 hours
   - **Investment Phase:** Integrated permit processing with sectoral agencies
   - **Post-Investment Phase:** Automated compliance monitoring and reporting

3. **Risk-Based Licensing Categories**
   - **Low Risk:** Automatic approval (maximum 1 day)
   - **Medium-Low Risk:** Simplified verification (maximum 7 days)
   - **Medium-High Risk:** Standard verification (maximum 20 days)
   - **High Risk:** Comprehensive assessment (maximum 25 days)
