#### **Integrated Social Security Architecture (Pasal 1-35)**

1. **Universal Healthcare Coverage (JKN-KIS)**
   - BPJS Kesehatan integration with regional healthcare facilities
   - Tiered healthcare service delivery system (Faskes I, II, III)
   - Digital health record integration and interoperability
   - Preventive healthcare program integration

2. **Employment Social Security (Jamsostek)**
   - **BPJS Ketenagakerjaan Components:**
     - Work accident insurance (JKK)
     - Death benefit insurance (JKM) 
     - Old age savings (JHT)
     - Pension insurance (JP)
   - Integrated contribution collection system
   - Digital claim processing and benefit distribution

3. **Social Assistance Integration**
   - **PKH (Family Hope Program) Coordination**
   - **BPNT (Non-Cash Food Assistance) Integration**
   - **KIP/PIP (Indonesia Smart Card/Program) Alignment**
   - **KIS (Indonesia Healthy Card) Universal Coverage**

3. **Service Delivery Integration**
   - Single window service for multiple programs

   - Automated eligibility assessment and renewal
