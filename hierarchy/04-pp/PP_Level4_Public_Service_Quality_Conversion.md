# PP LEVEL 4 CONVERSION: PUBLIC SERVICE QUALITY STANDARDS

### **COMPLETE PP TEXT ANALYSIS**

**Official Title:** Peraturan Pemerintah tentang Pelaksanaan Undang-Undang Nomor 25 Tahun 2009 tentang Pelayanan Publik

**Legal Foundation:** UU No. 25/2009 (Public Service Law), integrated with digital transformation mandates

### **CORE REGULATORY FRAMEWORK**

#### **Service Quality Standards (Pasal 1-20)**
1. **Minimum Service Standards (SPM)**
   - Response time: Maximum 30 minutes for initial contact
   - Resolution time: 80% of services completed within declared timeframe
   - Accessibility: Services available through minimum 3 channels

2. **Citizen Engagement Requirements**
   - Public consultation mandatory for service design changes
   - Complaint mechanism with 5-day maximum response time
   - Satisfaction survey requirement (quarterly minimum)

### **SERVICE DELIVERY MATRIX**

#### **Priority Service Categories**

**CATEGORY A - Essential Services:**
- Identity documents (KTP, Passport, Driving License)
- Business licensing (OSS integration mandatory)
- Healthcare services (BPJS coordination)
- Education services (school registration, scholarships)

**CATEGORY B - Supporting Services:**
- Tax services (integration with DJP systems)
- Social welfare distribution
- Infrastructure permits
- Environmental clearances

### **IMPLEMENTATION ANALYSIS**

**LEAD MINISTRY:**
- **Kemenpan-RB** (State Apparatus Utilization and Bureaucratic Reform)
  - Service standard setting and monitoring
  - Performance evaluation system management
  - Innovation program coordination

**COORDINATING MINISTRIES:**
- **Kemkominfo:** Digital service platform integration
- **Kemendagri:** Regional government capacity building
- **Kemenkeu:** Budget allocation optimization for service improvement

**SECTORAL MINISTRIES:**
Each ministry responsible for sector-specific service standards:
- Kemenkes: Healthcare service quality standards
- Kemendikbud: Education service delivery standards
- Kemenaker: Employment service quality metrics

#### **Regional Impact Assessment**

**REGIONAL IMPLEMENTATION STRATEGY:**

**TIER 1 REGIONS** (Major Urban Centers):
- **Jakarta, Surabaya, Bandung, Medan**
- Full integrated service centers (Mal Pelayanan Publik)
- 24/7 digital service availability
- Multilingual service support (Indonesian + English + Local Language)

**TIER 2 REGIONS** (Provincial Capitals):
- **34 Provincial capitals**
- Integrated service centers with extended hours
- Bilingual service support (Indonesian + Local Language)
- Mobile service unit deployment

**TIER 3 REGIONS** (Remote Areas):
- **Rural districts and remote islands**
- Mobile service delivery prioritization
- Community-based service points
- Simplified procedure implementation

### **QUALITY ASSURANCE FRAMEWORK**

#### **Performance Measurement System**

**Key Performance Indicators:**
1. **Service Delivery Time:** 90% of services completed within standard time
2. **Citizen Satisfaction Rate:** Minimum 85% satisfaction score
3. **First-Call Resolution Rate:** 75% of inquiries resolved on first contact
4. **Digital Adoption Rate:** 70% of services accessed digitally

**Quality Control Mechanism:**
- Mystery shopper program (quarterly assessments)
- Citizen feedback integration system
- Staff competency certification requirements
- Continuous improvement program implementation

### **CITIZEN ENGAGEMENT PROTOCOLS**

#### **Participation Mechanisms**
1. **Public Consultation Process:**
   - 30-day minimum consultation period for major changes
   - Multi-channel feedback collection (online, offline, mobile)
   - Feedback analysis and response publication requirement

2. **Complaint Resolution System:**
   - SP4N-Lapor integration mandatory
   - 5-day maximum response time for initial acknowledgment
   - 30-day maximum resolution time for complex cases
   - Escalation mechanism to ombudsman if unresolved

### **ENFORCEMENT AND SANCTIONS**

**Compliance Monitoring:**
- Annual service quality audit by independent assessors
- Real-time performance monitoring dashboard
- Citizen satisfaction survey requirement (quarterly)

**Penalty Structure:**
- Warning letters for minor violations
- Budget reduction for persistent non-compliance
- Leadership accountability measures for systematic failures

### **INNOVATION INCENTIVE FRAMEWORK**

**Service Innovation Programs:**
- Annual public service innovation competition
- Best practice sharing mechanism between regions
- Technology adoption incentive schemes
- Public-private partnership facilitation
