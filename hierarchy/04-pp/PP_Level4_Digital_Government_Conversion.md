# PP LEVEL 4 CONVERSION: DIGITAL GOVERNMENT IMPLEMENTATION

### **COMPLETE PP TEXT ANALYSIS**

**Official Title:** Peraturan Pemerintah tentang Penyelenggaraan Sistem dan Transaksi Elektronik dan subsequent updates for digital government implementation

**Legal Foundation:** UU No. 11/2008 jo UU No. 19/2016 (ITE Law), Presidential Instructions on E-Government

### **CORE REGULATORY FRAMEWORK**

#### **Digital Government Architecture (Pasal 1-15)**
1. **E-Government Platform Integration**
   - Single Sign-On (SSO) mandatory for all government services
   - National data center standardization requirements
   - API interoperability protocols between ministries

2. **Digital Service Delivery Standards**
   - Maximum 3-click rule for citizen services
   - 24/7 availability requirement (99.9% uptime)
   - Multi-channel access (web, mobile, kiosk)

### **IMPLEMENTATION ANALYSIS**

#### **Ministry Coordination Requirements**

**PRIMARY COORDINATING MINISTRIES:**
- **Kemkominfo** (Digital Infrastructure Lead)
  - National broadband deployment oversight
  - Cybersecurity framework implementation
  - Digital literacy program coordination

- **Kemenpan-RB** (Public Service Reform)
  - Service standard optimization (SPM integration)
  - Bureaucratic reform digital transformation
  - Performance measurement system integration

- **BSSN** (Cybersecurity Agency)
  - Security framework implementation
  - Incident response coordination
  - Digital forensics capability building

**SUPPORTING MINISTRIES:**
- Kemenkeu: Digital payment integration, e-procurement
- Kemendagri: Regional government digital capability
- Kemenkes: Health information system integration

#### **Regional Impact Assessment**

**PROVINCIAL LEVEL IMPLEMENTATION:**
- **High Capacity Regions** (DKI Jakarta, Jawa Barat, Jawa Tengah)
  - Full digital service implementation by 2024
  - Smart city integration requirements
  - Public-private partnership facilitation

- **Medium Capacity Regions** (Sumatra, Kalimantan major cities)
  - Phased implementation over 2024-2025
  - Infrastructure development priority
  - Capacity building program integration

- **Remote/Eastern Regions** (Papua, NTT, Maluku)
  - Mobile-first service delivery approach
  - Satellite connectivity utilization
  - Community-based service point establishment

### **KEY PERFORMANCE INDICATORS**

2. **Citizen Satisfaction Index:** >4.0/5.0 for digital services
3. **Processing Time Reduction:** 70% average reduction in service completion time
4. **Digital Inclusion Rate:** 75% of citizens using government digital services
