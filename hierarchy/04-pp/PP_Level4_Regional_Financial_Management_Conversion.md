### **FISCAL DECENTRALIZATION MECHANISMS**

#### **Revenue Sharing Formula Optimization**

**NATURAL RESOURCE REVENUE SHARING:**
- **Oil and Gas:** 15.5% to producing regions
- **Mining:** 80% to producing regions (after central deduction)
- **Forestry:** 80% to regional governments
- **Fisheries:** 100% to regional management

**TAX REVENUE SHARING:**
- **Income Tax (PPh):** 20% to regional governments
- **Land and Building Tax:** 100% to regional governments
- **Vehicle Tax:** 100% to regional governments
- **Regional Tax Innovation:** New tax type authority with central approval
