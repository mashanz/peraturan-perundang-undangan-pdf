## I. FOREST CRIMES FRAMEWORK
### **Critical for Indonesian Rainforest Protection**

#### **Article 187A - Illegal Deforestation (Comprehensive)**
- **Offense:** Deliberately destroying forest areas without proper permits
- **Penalty:** 5-12 years imprisonment + Rp 2-10 billion fine
- **Aggravated Form:** 
  - Protected forest areas: 8-15 years + Rp 5-20 billion
  - Peat bog destruction: 10-20 years + Rp 10-50 billion
- **Corporate Liability:** Company dissolution + environmental restoration costs

#### **Article 187B - Forest Burning Crimes**
- **Offense:** Intentional forest burning or slash-and-burn practices
- **Base Penalty:** 7-15 years imprisonment + Rp 5-25 billion fine
- **Aggravated Circumstances:**
  - Trans-boundary haze pollution: Additional 5-10 years
  - During drought/high-risk periods: Additional fine 100% increase
  - Impact on human health/aviation: 12-20 years imprisonment

#### **Article 187C - Illegal Logging Operations**
- **Individual Offense:** 3-8 years + Rp 1-5 billion fine
- **Corporate Operations:** 
  - Company fine: Rp 10-100 billion
  - License revocation mandatory
  - Restoration fund establishment required
- **Organized Crime:** 10-25 years imprisonment (RICO-style provisions)
