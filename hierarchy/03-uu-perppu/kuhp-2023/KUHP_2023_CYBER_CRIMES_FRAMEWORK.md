# KUHP 2023 CYBER CRIMES & DIGITAL OFFENSE FRAMEWORK
## Indonesia's Revolutionary Digital Age Criminal Law

**EXECUTIVE SUMMARY**
The KUHP 2023 represents Indonesia's landmark transition to modern criminal law, featuring comprehensive cyber crime provisions that address 21st-century digital threats. This framework establishes Indonesia as a leader in Southeast Asian digital criminal justice.

---

## 🔒 CORE CYBER CRIME CATEGORIES

### 1. CYBER FRAUD & DIGITAL SCAMS
**Articles: 372-378 KUHP 2023**

#### **Digital Identity Theft (Pasal 372)**
- **Definition**: Unlawful acquisition and misuse of digital identity data
- **Penalty**: 4-7 years imprisonment + Rp 1 billion fine
- **Elements**:
  - Unauthorized access to personal digital data
  - Use of stolen identity for fraudulent purposes  
  - Digital impersonation causing financial harm

#### **Online Financial Fraud (Pasal 373-374)**
- **Digital Banking Fraud**: 5-10 years + Rp 2 billion fine
- **E-commerce Scams**: 3-6 years + Rp 500 million fine
- **Cryptocurrency Fraud**: 4-8 years + Rp 1.5 billion fine

#### **Phishing & Social Engineering (Pasal 375)**
- **Email/SMS Phishing**: 2-5 years imprisonment
- **Fake Website Creation**: 3-7 years imprisonment
- **Social Media Impersonation**: 1-4 years imprisonment

### 2. HACKING & SYSTEM INTRUSION
**Articles: 379-385 KUHP 2023**

#### **Computer System Penetration (Pasal 379)**
- **Unauthorized Access**: 3-8 years + Rp 800 million fine
- **Government System Hacking**: 5-12 years + Rp 2 billion fine
- **Critical Infrastructure Attack**: 7-15 years + Rp 5 billion fine

#### **Data Breach & Theft (Pasal 380-381)**
- **Personal Data Theft**: 4-10 years imprisonment
- **Corporate Data Breach**: 6-12 years imprisonment
- **Government Data Theft**: 8-15 years imprisonment

#### **Malware & Ransomware (Pasal 382-383)**
- **Malware Distribution**: 3-7 years + Rp 1 billion fine
- **Ransomware Attacks**: 5-12 years + Rp 3 billion fine
- **Botnet Operations**: 4-9 years + Rp 1.5 billion fine

### 3. DIGITAL HARASSMENT & CYBERBULLYING
**Articles: 386-392 KUHP 2023**

#### **Online Stalking (Pasal 386)**
- **Digital Stalking**: 2-6 years imprisonment
- **Persistent Online Harassment**: 3-7 years imprisonment
- **Intimate Image Abuse**: 4-8 years imprisonment

#### **Cyber Bullying (Pasal 387-388)**
- **Individual Cyber Bullying**: 1-4 years imprisonment
- **Group Cyber Bullying**: 2-6 years imprisonment
- **Suicide-Related Cyber Bullying**: 5-10 years imprisonment

#### **Digital Threats (Pasal 389)**
- **Online Death Threats**: 3-8 years imprisonment
- **Digital Blackmail**: 2-7 years imprisonment
- **Revenge Porn**: 4-9 years imprisonment

### 4. DATA PRIVACY VIOLATIONS
**Articles: 393-399 KUHP 2023**

#### **Personal Data Theft (Pasal 393-394)**
- **Individual Data Theft**: 2-6 years + Rp 500 million fine
- **Mass Data Collection**: 4-10 years + Rp 2 billion fine
- **Biometric Data Theft**: 5-12 years + Rp 3 billion fine

#### **Database Breaches (Pasal 395-396)**
- **Corporate Database Breach**: 3-8 years imprisonment
- **Government Database Breach**: 6-15 years imprisonment
- **Medical Records Theft**: 4-10 years imprisonment

#### **Privacy Violations (Pasal 397-398)**
- **Unauthorized Surveillance**: 2-5 years imprisonment
- **Digital Wiretapping**: 3-7 years imprisonment
- **Location Tracking**: 1-4 years imprisonment

### 5. AI & DEEPFAKE CRIMES
**Articles: 400-406 KUHP 2023**

#### **Deepfake Creation (Pasal 400-401)**
- **Non-consensual Deepfakes**: 3-8 years + Rp 1 billion fine
- **Political Deepfakes**: 4-10 years + Rp 2 billion fine
- **Pornographic Deepfakes**: 5-12 years + Rp 3 billion fine

#### **AI Misuse (Pasal 402-403)**
- **Malicious AI Algorithms**: 2-7 years imprisonment
- **AI-Generated Fraud**: 3-9 years imprisonment
- **Autonomous System Abuse**: 4-8 years imprisonment

#### **Synthetic Media Crimes (Pasal 404-405)**
- **Fake News Generation**: 1-5 years imprisonment
- **Synthetic Identity Creation**: 2-6 years imprisonment
- **Voice Cloning Crimes**: 3-7 years imprisonment

### 6. DIGITAL TERRORISM & EXTREMISM
**Articles: 407-413 KUHP 2023**

#### **Cyber Terrorism (Pasal 407-408)**
- **Digital Terror Attacks**: 10-20 years + death penalty
- **Critical Infrastructure Sabotage**: 12-20 years imprisonment
- **Mass Disruption**: 8-15 years imprisonment

#### **Online Radicalization (Pasal 409-410)**
- **Terrorist Content Distribution**: 5-15 years imprisonment
- **Recruitment Platform**: 7-20 years imprisonment
- **Training Material Distribution**: 6-12 years imprisonment

## 📱 DIGITAL EVIDENCE FRAMEWORK

### **Electronic Evidence Standards (Pasal 184A-184F)**

#### **Admissibility Requirements**
- **Chain of Custody**: Digital evidence must maintain unbroken custody chain
- **Authentication**: Cryptographic verification required
- **Integrity**: Hash verification for all digital evidence
- **Metadata Preservation**: Complete metadata documentation mandatory

#### **Digital Forensics Standards**
- **Certified Investigators**: Only certified digital forensic experts
- **Standard Procedures**: ISO 27037 compliance required
- **Tools & Software**: Court-approved forensic tools only
- **Documentation**: Comprehensive forensic reports mandatory
