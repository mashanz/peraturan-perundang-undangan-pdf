# 02-NATIONAL-LAWS - Laws and Emergency Laws
## ⚖️ National Legislation and Emergency Regulations

This directory contains national laws passed by the Indonesian Parliament (DPR) and emergency laws issued by the President.

### Directory Structure
- **uu-laws/** - Laws (Undang-Undang) 
- **perppu/** - Emergency Laws (Peraturan Pemerintah Pengganti Undang-Undang)
- **kuhp-2023/** - Criminal Code 2023 (Kitab Undang-Undang Hukum Pidana)

### Document Types
- **UU (Laws)**: Legislation passed by Parliament with presidential approval
- **PERPPU (Emergency Laws)**: Presidential emergency regulations with force of law
- **KUHP**: The comprehensive Criminal Code of Indonesia

### Hierarchy Level: **LEVEL 2** ⭐⭐⭐⭐
These documents have very high legal authority, second only to constitutional documents.

### Filename Convention
- Laws: `uu-[number]-[year].pdf`
- Emergency Laws: `perppu-[number]-[year].pdf` 
- Criminal Code: `kuhp-[section]-[year].pdf`

### Current Statistics
- **496M** total size
- Includes laws from 1984 to 2025
- Major recent additions: UU No. 6/2023 (Criminal Code), UU No. 11/2020 (Job Creation)

### Legal Authority
These regulations can override all lower-level regulations but must conform to constitutional principles.
