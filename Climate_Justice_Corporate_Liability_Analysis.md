#### **No-Intent Requirement**
- **Revolutionary Change:** Corporations liable for environmental damage regardless of intent
- **Rationale:** Environmental damage prevention priority over corporate protection
- **Standard:** If corporate activity causes environmental harm, liability automatic

#### **Unprecedented Penalty Structure**
- **Percentage-Based Fines:** 1-10% of annual global turnover
- **Minimum Floor:** Rp 10 billion (ensures meaningful impact on small companies)
- **Maximum Cap:** Rp 1 trillion (severe deterrent for major corporations)
- **Asset Forfeiture:** Environmental crime proceeds subject to confiscation

### **Executive Personal Liability (Article 192B)**
- **CEO Criminal Responsibility:** Personal liability for corporate environmental crimes
- **Director Disqualification:** 5-25 year industry bans for environmental crime executives  
- **Piercing Corporate Veil:** Individual liability despite corporate structure

### **Restorative Corporate Justice (Article 192C)**

- **Long-term Monitoring:** 10-25 year environmental monitoring obligations
- **Community Compensation:** Direct payment to affected communities
