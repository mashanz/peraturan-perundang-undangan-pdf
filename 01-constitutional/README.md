# 01-CONSTITUTIONAL - Constitutional Documents
## 🏛️ Fundamental Laws and Constitutional Framework

This directory contains the highest-level legal documents in the Indonesian legal hierarchy:

### Directory Structure
- **uud-1945/** - Constitution of 1945 (Undang-Undang Dasar 1945)
- **tap-mpr/** - MPR Decrees (Ketetapan MPR)
- **mk-decisions/** - Constitutional Court Decisions (Putusan Mahkamah Konstitusi)

### Document Types
- **UUD 1945**: The fundamental constitution of the Republic of Indonesia
- **TAP MPR**: People's Consultative Assembly (Majelis Permusyawaratan Rakyat) decrees
- **MK Decisions**: Constitutional Court rulings on constitutional matters

### Hierarchy Level: **LEVEL 1** ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐
These are the supreme legal documents that form the foundation of all other Indonesian law.

### Filename Convention
- Constitution: `uud-1945-amendment-[number]-[year].pdf`
- MPR Decrees: `tap-mpr-[number]-[year].pdf`
- MK Decisions: `mk-decision-[number]-[year].pdf`

### Legal Authority
All documents in this directory have **supreme legal authority** and cannot be contradicted by lower-level regulations.
