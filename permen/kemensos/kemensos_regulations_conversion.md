## PRIORITY REGULATION CATEGORIES IDENTIFIED

### 1. SOCIAL PROTECTION SYSTEMS

#### **Regulation Framework: Program Keluarga Harapan (PKH)**
- **Legal Basis:** Presidential Decree on Conditional Cash Transfer
- **Implementation:** Ministry of Social Affairs coordination with local governments
- **Target Beneficiaries:** Poor and vulnerable households with children, pregnant women, elderly
- **Integration Points:**
  - DTKS (Unified Social Welfare Database)
  - Bansos integration
  - Health and education conditionalities

#### **Social Assistance (Bansos) Framework**
- **Coverage:** Basic Social Assistance, Rice for Prosperity (Rastra), Social Assistance Cards
- **Implementation Structure:** Central-Regional-Village level coordination
- **Beneficiary Targeting:** Bottom 40% population quintiles

### 2. DISABILITY RIGHTS & SERVICES

#### **UU No. 8 Tahun 2016 tentang Penyandang Disabilitas**
- **Revolutionary Framework:** Rights-based approach replacing charity model
- **Key Provisions:**
  - Accessibility requirements for public facilities
  - Employment quotas (1% private sector, 2% public sector)
  - Inclusive education guarantees
  - Social rehabilitation services
- **Implementation Challenges:** Regional capacity, budget allocation, awareness

#### **Disability Benefits System**
- **Types:** Monthly disability allowance, accessibility equipment, rehabilitation services
- **Integration:** Coordination with Health Ministry, Education Ministry, Labor Ministry

### 3. CHILD PROTECTION & WELFARE

#### **Child Welfare Services Framework**
- **Legal Foundation:** Law No. 35/2014 on Child Protection (amendment to Law 23/2002)
- **Institutional Structure:**
  - KPPPA (Women Empowerment and Child Protection Ministry) coordination
  - Kemensos service delivery
  - P2TP2A (Integrated Service Centers)

#### **Child Social Protection Programs**
- **PKH Child Component:** Education and health conditionalities
- **Child Social Services:** Protection, rehabilitation, family strengthening
- **Adoption Procedures:** Streamlined processes, priority to Indonesian families

### 4. ELDERLY CARE SERVICES

#### **Elderly Social Protection Framework**
- **Legal Basis:** Law No. 13/1998 on Elderly Welfare
- **Service Components:**
  - Elderly social assistance (ASLUT)
  - Home-based care programs
  - Elderly-friendly facility standards
  - Community-based elderly care

#### **Integration with Healthcare**
- **JKN Coordination:** Social health insurance alignment
- **Long-term Care:** Family-based care support systems

### 5. SOCIAL REHABILITATION

#### **Drug Rehabilitation System**
- **Legal Framework:** Coordination with BNN (National Narcotics Agency)
- **Service Model:** Community-based rehabilitation, halfway houses
- **Reintegration Programs:** Skills training, job placement support

#### **Homeless Services**
- **Intervention Model:** Street outreach, temporary shelter, reintegration
- **Urban Focus:** Jakarta, Surabaya, Medan primary locations

### 6. DISASTER SOCIAL RESPONSE

#### **Emergency Social Assistance Framework**
- **Rapid Response Mechanism:** 24-hour activation protocols
- **Coordination Structure:**
  - BNPB (National Disaster Management Agency) partnership
  - Regional BPBD coordination
  - Village-level implementation

#### **Displacement Support Systems**
- **Temporary Shelter Standards:** Basic needs provision, dignity protection
- **Recovery Programs:** Livelihood restoration, community infrastructure

### 7. COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT

#### **Rural Development Integration**
- **Village Fund Coordination:** Social programs alignment with village development
- **Community Participation:** Musrenbang (development planning meetings) integration

#### **Social Empowerment Programs**
- **KUBE (Joint Business Groups):** Microenterprise development
- **LKS (Social Welfare Institutions):** Community organization strengthening

### 8. SOCIAL WORK PROFESSIONAL STANDARDS

#### **Certification Framework**
- **Professional Standards:** Competency-based certification
- **Education Requirements:** Bachelor's degree in Social Work
- **Continuing Education:** Annual professional development requirements

#### **Ethics and Practice Standards**
- **Code of Ethics:** Client confidentiality, professional boundaries
- **Supervision Requirements:** Licensed social worker oversight
