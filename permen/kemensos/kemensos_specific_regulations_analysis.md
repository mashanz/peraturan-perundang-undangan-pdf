## CORE REGULATORY FRAMEWORK IDENTIFICATION

### **TIER 1: FOUNDATIONAL LAWS**

#### **1. UU No. 8 Tahun 2016 tentang Penyandang Disabilitas**
**Full Title:** Undang-Undang Republik Indonesia Nomor 8 Tahun 2016 tentang Penyandang Disabilitas

**REVOLUTIONARY FRAMEWORK ANALYSIS:**
- **Paradigm Shift:** From charity-based to rights-based approach
- **Legal Status:** Replaces and modernizes UU No. 4/1997
- **Implementation Timeline:** 5-year phase implementation (2016-2021, extended to 2025)

**KEY PROVISIONS:**
1. **Article 5:** Definition expansion - physical, intellectual, mental, sensory disabilities
2. **Article 9:** Right to accessibility in all public facilities
3. **Article 11:** Employment rights - 1% private sector, 2% public sector quota
4. **Article 40-43:** Social rehabilitation and habilitation services
5. **Article 142:** Criminal sanctions for discrimination

**IMPLEMENTATION MECHANISMS:**
- **NAP-HAK (National Action Plan for Human Rights):** Integration required
- **Regional Implementation:** Provincial and District/City regulations required
- **Budget Allocation:** Central, regional, village fund coordination
- **Monitoring Body:** National Commission on Disability Rights

**Implementation Update Requirements:** Ministerial regulations for operationalization

**PRIORITY UPDATE AREAS:**
