**Primary Jurisdictions:**
- Environmental Impact Assessment (AMDAL) - UU 32/2009
- Climate Change Mitigation - PP 79/2014, UU 16/2016
- Waste Management - UU 18/2008
- Water Quality Protection - UU 17/2019
- Biodiversity Conservation (Species) - UU 5/1990
- Green Industry Standards - UU 3/2014
- International Environmental Cooperation - UU 16/2016

#### Ministry of Forestry (Kementerian Kehutanan)
**Primary Jurisdictions:**
- Forest Management & Protection - UU 41/1999 (amended UU 11/2020)
- Conservation Areas - UU 5/1990 (habitat aspects)
- REDD+ Implementation
- Social Forestry Programs
- Timber Legality Verification (SVLK)

## CRITICAL IMPLEMENTATION GAPS

### 1. Enforcement Capacity Constraints
**Problem:** Limited human resources and technical capacity for monitoring compliance
**Impact:** High rate of regulatory violations, especially in remote areas
**Solutions:**
- Digital monitoring systems expansion
- Capacity building for local enforcement officers
- Community-based monitoring programs
- Enhanced inter-agency coordination

### 2. Regulatory Fragmentation
**Problem:** Overlapping jurisdictions between agencies post-ministry split
**Impact:** Coordination delays, regulatory uncertainty for businesses
**Solutions:**
- Clear jurisdiction mapping and MOUs
- Joint implementation protocols
- Unified digital platforms
- Regular inter-ministerial coordination meetings

### 3. Technology Transfer Barriers
**Problem:** Limited access to clean technology, high costs, inadequate technical support
**Impact:** Slow green transition, continued reliance on polluting technologies
**Solutions:**
- National clean technology transfer center
- Subsidized financing for green technology
- Technical assistance programs
- International partnership enhancement

## INDONESIA'S CLIMATE COMMITMENTS INTEGRATION

### NDC Implementation Status (2024 Assessment)

#### Unconditional Target (29% reduction by 2030)
**Energy Sector:** 45% progress - renewable energy deployment accelerating
**Forest & Land Use:** 60% progress - deforestation reduction showing results
**Waste Sector:** 25% progress - infrastructure development lagging
**Industrial Sector:** 35% progress - green industry transition slow
**Transportation:** 20% progress - limited clean transport adoption

#### Conditional Target (41% with international support)
**Funding Gap:** $15 billion needed for conditional target achievement
**Technology Needs:** Focus on CCUS, advanced renewables, green hydrogen
**Capacity Building:** Enhanced MRV systems, climate planning expertise

### FOLU Net Sink 2030 Strategy
**Target:** Forest, Agriculture, and Other Land Use (FOLU) sector to achieve net sink by 2030
**Key Measures:**
- Zero net deforestation by 2028
- Peatland restoration (2.6 million hectares)
- Mangrove rehabilitation (600,000 hectares)
- Sustainable agriculture intensification
- Enhanced forest carbon sequestration

**Implementation Challenges:**
- Balancing development needs with conservation
- Ensuring smallholder farmer livelihoods
- Coordinating across multiple sectors and levels of government
- Monitoring and verification system enhancement

## DEFORESTATION PREVENTION ENHANCED STRATEGY

### Current Framework Assessment
**Legal Foundation:** Strong (UU 41/1999, various presidential instructions)
**Enforcement Capacity:** Moderate (satellite monitoring, but limited field presence)
**Community Engagement:** Good (social forestry program expansion)
**International Support:** High (Norway partnership, REDD+ programs)

### Palm Oil Sustainability Integration
**ISPO Standard:** Mandatory for all palm oil companies (2019)
**No New Permits Policy:** Primary forest and peat areas protected
**Supply Chain Transparency:** Traceability systems implementation
**Smallholder Support:** Certification assistance and technical support

**Enhancement Recommendations:**
1. **Strengthen Zero Deforestation Implementation**
   - Expand moratorium to cover more forest types
   - Enhance real-time monitoring systems
   - Improve rapid response to illegal activities
   - Strengthen legal framework for corporate accountability

2. **Community-Based Forest Management**
   - Accelerate social forestry permit issuance
   - Provide technical and financial support for forest communities
   - Develop alternative livelihood programs
   - Enhance traditional forest governance recognition

3. **International Cooperation Enhancement**
   - Expand partnerships beyond Norway (EU, private sector)
   - Develop innovative financing mechanisms (green bonds, carbon credits)
   - Strengthen regional cooperation on illegal logging
   - Enhance technology transfer for sustainable forest management
