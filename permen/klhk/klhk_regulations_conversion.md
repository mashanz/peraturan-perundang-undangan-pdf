## PRIORITY REGULATION CONVERSION

### 1. ENVIRONMENTAL IMPACT ASSESSMENT (AMDAL)

#### Regulation: UU No. 32/2009 - Environmental Protection and Management
**Legal Framework:**
- **Full Title:** Undang-Undang Nomor 32 Tahun 2009 tentang Perlindungan dan Pengelolaan Lingkungan Hidup
- **Status:** Active - Replaces UU No. 23/1997
- **Scope:** Comprehensive environmental protection framework

**Key Provisions:**
1. **AMDAL Requirements (Articles 22-33)**
   - Mandatory for business plans with significant environmental impact
   - Environmental Impact Assessment (AMDAL) process requirements
   - Environmental Management Plan (RKL) and Monitoring Plan (RPL)
   - Public participation in AMDAL process

2. **Environmental Licensing (Articles 36-40)**
   - Environmental permits required before business/activity permits
   - Integration with One Stop Service (OSS) system
   - Periodic evaluation and renewal requirements

3. **Compliance Monitoring (Articles 71-84)**
   - Administrative sanctions: warnings, license suspension/revocation
   - Criminal sanctions for severe violations
   - Civil liability for environmental damage

**Implementation Framework:**
- **Lead Agency:** Ministry of Environment (now split from KLHK)
- **Provincial/Regional Roles:** AMDAL evaluation for regional jurisdiction
- **Integration with:** OSS system, sectoral licensing, spatial planning
- **Timeline:** AMDAL process typically 75-105 working days

**Environmental System Integration:**
- Links to air quality standards (PP 41/1999)
- Water quality management coordination
- Waste management integration
- Climate impact consideration mandatory since 2021

**International Climate Cooperation:**
- Aligns with Paris Agreement commitments
- Supports NDC implementation through environmental safeguards
- Integrates climate resilience assessment
- Links to REDD+ safeguards for forestry projects

### 2. FOREST MANAGEMENT & PROTECTION

#### Regulation: UU No. 41/1999 - Forestry Law (as amended by UU No. 11/2020 Job Creation Law)
**Legal Framework:**
- **Full Title:** Undang-Undang Nomor 41 Tahun 1999 tentang Kehutanan
- **Status:** Active with Job Creation Law amendments
- **Scope:** Comprehensive forest governance and sustainable management

**Key Provisions:**
1. **Forest Classification (Articles 6-9)**
   - Conservation forests, protection forests, production forests
   - State forest vs. customary forest recognition
   - Forest area designation and boundary demarcation

2. **Sustainable Forest Management (Articles 20-28)**
   - Forest Management Units (KPH) establishment
   - Sustainable yield calculations
   - Forest certification requirements (SVLK - Indonesian Legal Wood)
   - Community forestry programs (Perhutanan Sosial)

3. **Forest Protection (Articles 44-50)**
   - Prohibition of illegal logging
   - Forest fire prevention and control
   - Protected species conservation
   - Law enforcement mechanisms

**Implementation Framework:**
- **Lead Agency:** Ministry of Forestry (post-2024 restructuring)
- **Regional Implementation:** Provincial forestry services, KPH management
- **Community Engagement:** Social forestry permits, customary forest recognition
- **Monitoring Systems:** SILK (Legal Wood Information System), satellite monitoring

**Environmental System Integration:**
- Watershed protection coordination with water management
- Biodiversity conservation through protected area networks
- Carbon storage and sequestration measurement
- Integration with spatial planning systems

**International Climate Cooperation:**
- REDD+ implementation framework
- Paris Agreement forest sector contributions
- Indonesia-Norway cooperation on deforestation
- FLEGT-VPA compliance for timber exports

### 3. CLIMATE CHANGE MITIGATION

#### Regulation: PP No. 79/2014 - National Energy Policy Implementation
**Legal Framework:**
- **Full Title:** Peraturan Pemerintah Nomor 79 Tahun 2014 tentang Kebijakan Energi Nasional
- **Status:** Active - Implements UU No. 30/2007 Energy Law
- **Scope:** National energy transition and renewable energy targets

**Key Provisions:**
1. **Renewable Energy Targets (Articles 11-15)**
   - 23% renewable energy by 2025, 31% by 2050
   - Priority for solar, wind, hydro, geothermal, bioenergy
   - Energy efficiency targets: 1% annually

2. **Carbon Emission Reduction (Articles 20-25)**
   - Fossil fuel reduction roadmap
   - Carbon pricing mechanism development
   - Energy sector emission monitoring

3. **Green Technology Development (Articles 16-19)**
   - Clean technology incentives
   - Research and development support
   - Technology transfer facilitation

**Implementation Framework:**
- **Lead Agencies:** Ministry of Energy and Mineral Resources, Ministry of Environment
- **Provincial Role:** Regional energy planning, renewable energy development
- **Private Sector Engagement:** Investment incentives, feed-in tariffs
- **Monitoring:** National energy balance, emission tracking systems

**Environmental System Integration:**
- Integration with environmental licensing for energy projects
- Coordination with forest conservation (bioenergy sustainability)
- Water resource management for hydropower
- Air quality improvement through clean energy transition

**International Climate Cooperation:**
- Paris Agreement NDC implementation (29% unconditional, 41% conditional emission reduction)
- Just Energy Transition Partnership (JETP) alignment
- Clean energy investment facilitation
- Technology transfer agreements

**Key Provisions:**
1. **Protected Area System (Articles 8-20)**

2. **Species Protection (Articles 21-35)**

3. **Habitat Conservation (Articles 13-19)**

   - Ecosystem restoration requirements
   - Buffer zone management
   - Community-based conservation

**Implementation Framework:**
- **Lead Agency:** Ministry of Forestry (conservation areas), Ministry of Environment (species protection)
- **Field Implementation:** National park authorities, conservation agencies
- **Law Enforcement:** Forest rangers, wildlife crime units, customs
- **Community Engagement:** Conservation agreements, ecotourism development

**Environmental System Integration:**

- Marine protected area coordination

**International Climate Cooperation:**

### 8. INTERNATIONAL ENVIRONMENTAL COOPERATION

#### Regulation: UU No. 16/2016 - Paris Agreement Ratification
**Legal Framework:**
- **Full Title:** Undang-Undang Nomor 16 Tahun 2016 tentang Pengesahan Paris Agreement
- **Status:** Active - Indonesia's climate commitment framework
- **Scope:** International climate cooperation and NDC implementation

**Key Provisions:**
1. **National Determined Contributions (NDCs)**
   - 29% unconditional emission reduction by 2030 (2010 baseline)
   - 41% conditional reduction with international support
   - Regular NDC updates every 5 years
   - Long-term low emission development strategy

2. **International Cooperation Mechanisms**
   - Climate finance mobilization
   - Technology transfer facilitation
   - Capacity building programs
   - Transparency and reporting systems

3. **Sectoral Implementation**
   - Energy sector transformation
   - Forest and land use (FOLU Net Sink 2030)
   - Industrial process improvement
   - Adaptation planning and implementation

**Implementation Framework:**
- **Lead Agency:** Ministry of Environment (climate change coordination)
- **Cross-sector Coordination:** All ministries with emission sources
- **Subnational Implementation:** Provincial and local climate action plans
- **Monitoring System:** National MRV (Measurement, Reporting, Verification)

**Environmental System Integration:**
- Integration with all environmental regulations
- Sectoral emission reduction strategies
- Adaptation and resilience building
- Sustainable development co-benefits

**International Climate Cooperation:**
- UNFCCC reporting and compliance
- G20 climate commitments
- ASEAN climate cooperation
- Bilateral climate partnerships (Norway, Japan, Germany)

## COMPREHENSIVE ENVIRONMENTAL SYSTEM ANALYSIS

### Current Regulatory Framework Assessment

**Strengths:**
1. **Comprehensive Legal Foundation:** Well-established primary laws covering all major environmental sectors
2. **Integration Mechanisms:** Cross-sectoral coordination requirements in most regulations
3. **International Alignment:** Strong integration with global environmental agreements
4. **Multi-level Governance:** Clear division of responsibilities between national, provincial, and local levels

**Implementation Challenges:**
1. **Enforcement Capacity:** Limited resources for monitoring and enforcement
2. **Coordination Gaps:** Overlapping jurisdictions between agencies
3. **Technology Transfer:** Barriers to clean technology adoption
4. **Community Engagement:** Limited meaningful participation in decision-making

### Climate Commitments Integration

**NDC Implementation Status:**
- **Energy Sector:** Renewable energy targets on track but require acceleration
- **Forest Sector:** FOLU Net Sink target ambitious, requires enhanced forest protection
- **Waste Sector:** Circular economy transition in early stages
- **Industrial Sector:** Green industry development progressing slowly

**International Cooperation Priorities:**
1. **Climate Finance:** $15 billion needed for conditional NDC target
2. **Technology Transfer:** Focus on renewable energy, efficiency, CCUS
3. **Capacity Building:** Enhanced MRV systems, climate planning
4. **Regional Cooperation:** ASEAN climate integration, transboundary cooperation

### Recommendations for System Enhancement

1. **Regulatory Consolidation:** Develop integrated environmental code to reduce overlap
2. **Digital Integration:** Implement comprehensive environmental data platform
3. **Enforcement Strengthening:** Increase inspection capacity and penalty effectiveness
4. **Community Empowerment:** Expand participatory governance mechanisms
5. **Green Finance:** Develop comprehensive climate finance framework
6. **Technology Innovation:** Establish environmental technology transfer center

## SPECIAL FOCUS AREAS

### Deforestation Prevention Strategy

**Current Framework:**
- Moratorium on new forest permits (since 2011, extended multiple times)
- One Map initiative for spatial planning integration
- SVLK timber legality verification system
- Social forestry program (12.7 million hectares target)

**Palm Oil Sustainability Integration:**
- Indonesian Sustainable Palm Oil (ISPO) standard mandatory
- No new permits in primary forest and peat areas
- Smallholder support and certification programs
- Supply chain transparency requirements

**Enhancement Needs:**
- Strengthen law enforcement against illegal logging
- Expand alternative livelihood programs for forest communities
- Improve satellite monitoring and rapid response systems
- Enhanced international cooperation on illegal timber trade

### Marine Plastic Waste Reduction

**National Action Plan (2018-2025):**
- 70% marine plastic waste reduction target
- Source reduction, improved waste management, circular economy
- Priority focus on 5 major cities and provinces
- International cooperation on waste management technology

**Regulatory Integration:**
- Extended producer responsibility for packaging
- Single-use plastic restrictions in tourist areas
- Waste-to-energy development promotion
- Marine protected area plastic pollution controls

**Implementation Status:**
- Progress varies significantly by region
- Limited infrastructure in remote coastal areas
- Growing private sector engagement
- Strong community-based cleanup programs
