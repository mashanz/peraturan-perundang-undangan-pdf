## PRIORITY AREA 1: HEALTHCARE STANDARDS & QUALITY

### 1.1 Peraturan Menteri Kesehatan No. 34 Tahun 2022
**Title:** Standar Nasional Akreditasi Rumah Sakit (National Hospital Accreditation Standards)

**Legal Framework:**
- **Authority:** Menteri Kesehatan RI
- **Legal Basis:** UU No. 44/2009 tentang Rumah Sakit
- **Status:** Active, replacing PMK No. 34/2017
- **Scope:** All hospitals in Indonesia (public, private, regional)

**Implementation Framework:**
- **Phase 1 (2022-2023):** Transition period for existing hospitals
- **Phase 2 (2024-2025):** Full compliance mandatory
- **Phase 3 (2026+):** Continuous improvement protocols

**JKN Integration:**
- BPJS Kesehatan requires accredited hospitals for partnership
- Tariff adjustments based on accreditation levels
- Quality indicators directly linked to JKN performance metrics

**WHO Coordination:**
- Aligned with WHO Global Patient Safety Standards
- International Quality Improvement Framework compliance
- Regional SEARO (South-East Asia Regional Office) coordination protocols

**Key Provisions:**
1. 15 accreditation standards across 4 categories
2. Patient safety as primary focus area
3. Digital health record integration requirements
4. Emergency preparedness protocols
5. Infection prevention and control standards

### 1.2 Peraturan Menteri Kesehatan No. 18 Tahun 2023
