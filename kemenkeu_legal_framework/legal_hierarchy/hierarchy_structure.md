# Indonesian Legal Hierarchy Structure

## Legal Authority Levels (Highest to Lowest)

### 1. Constitutional Level
- **UUD 1945** - Constitution of the Republic of Indonesia
- **MPR Decrees (TAP MPR)** - People's Consultative Assembly Decrees

### 2. Parliamentary Level  
- **UU (Undang-Undang)** - Acts of Parliament
  - Primary legislation
  - Requires DPR approval
  - Can delegate authority to lower regulations

### 3. Government Level
- **PP (Peraturan Pemerintah)** - Government Regulations
  - Implementing regulations for UU
  - Issued by President
  - Requires ministerial coordination

### 4. Presidential Level
- **Perpres (Peraturan Presiden)** - Presidential Regulations
  - Administrative matters
  - Government organization
  - Policy implementation

### 5. Ministerial Level
- **PMK (Peraturan Menteri Keuangan)** - Ministry of Finance Regulations
  - Technical implementation
  - Operational procedures
  - Specific ministry authority

### 6. Director General Level
- **PER (Peraturan Direktur Jenderal)** - Director General Regulations
  - Detailed technical procedures
  - Implementation guidelines
  - Operational instructions

### 7. Director/Head Level
- **SE (Surat Edaran)** - Circulars
- **KMK (Keputusan Menteri Keuangan)** - Ministry of Finance Decisions
- **KEP (Keputusan)** - Decisions

## Authority Relationships

### Delegation Chain
```
UUD 1945 → UU → PP → Perpres → PMK → PER → SE/KMK
```

### Validation Requirements
- Each level must cite proper higher authority
- "MENGINGAT" clauses must reference enabling legislation
- Cannot contradict higher-level regulations
- Must stay within delegated scope

## PMK Specific Authority

### Common PMK Categories
1. **Tax Regulations** - Implementation of tax laws
2. **Budget and Finance** - State budget implementation
3. **Customs and Excise** - Trade and border control
4. **State Assets** - Government property management
5. **Financial Markets** - Capital market supervision
6. **Treasury Management** - Cash and debt management

### Typical PMK Structure
```
MENGINGAT:
- UU (enabling act)
- PP (government regulation)
- Perpres (if applicable)

MEMUTUSKAN:
- Substantive provisions
- Implementation procedures
- Sanctions and penalties
- Effective date

PENUTUP:
- Revocation clauses
- Transitional provisions
- Effective date confirmation
```

## Cross-Reference Requirements

### Mandatory Citations in PMK
- Primary UU that grants authority
- Relevant PP that delegates implementation
- Any superseded PMK (revocation clauses)
- Related PMK that interact with subject matter

### International Considerations
- Treaty implementation requirements
- WTO compliance obligations
- ASEAN agreement alignment
- Bilateral tax agreement provisions
